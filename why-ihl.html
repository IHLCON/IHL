Six Reasons to Choose IHL

What makes International House of Law different — and why leading organisations worldwide trust us with their most sensitive matters.

01

Multi-Jurisdictional Expertise

With offices in The Hague (Netherlands) and Vancouver (Canada), IHL operates at the intersection of civil law, common law, and Sharia-based legal systems. Our lawyers advise clients in over 30 countries, navigating jurisdictional complexity with precision and cultural intelligence.

02

Arbitration at Our Core

Arbitration is more than just a practice area — it is at the heart of who we are. Our deep expertise in international dispute resolution sets us apart from general commercial law firms. We bring rigour, strategy, and tested experience to every arbitration matter we handle.

03

Full-Spectrum Services

From dispute resolution and contract drafting to global business expansion and ESG strategy, IHL covers every dimension of your international legal needs — all under one roof. Clients benefit from coordinated advice that connects legal and business perspectives seamlessly.

04

Expert Opinions in Islamic & Iranian Law

We deliver highly specialised legal opinions in Iranian law, Islamic law, and other Sharia-based legal systems — a rare and sought-after competence in international legal practice. Our expertise is recognised in courts and arbitration panels across multiple jurisdictions.

05

Proactive Risk Management

We don't just react to legal problems — we anticipate them. Our dispute resolution background makes us exceptional contract advisors: we draft and review agreements with a litigator's eye, identifying weak points before they become costly disputes.

06

Trusted by Global Clients

ZTE, Persia Bank London, Ahli United Bank Bahrain, and more than a dozen other leading organisations trust IHL with their most sensitive matters. Our client relationships are built on discretion, responsiveness, and consistently excellent outcomes.
