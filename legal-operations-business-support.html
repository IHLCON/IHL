At International House of Law, our Legal Operations & Business Support service gives organisations the tools, processes, and expert guidance to run their legal function with the same discipline they apply to the rest of the business. We support companies at every stage: those building a legal operations function for the first time, those looking to reduce reliance on expensive outside counsel, and those whose in-house teams need specialist capacity or senior advisory input. The result is a leaner, more controlled, and more predictable legal function — without the overhead of a full in-house department.

01
Centralised Trackable Resolved

Matter Management & Legal Oversight

Keeping track of open legal matters across contracts, disputes, regulatory filings, and internal projects is harder than it should be. Without a single source of oversight, deadlines are missed, costs escalate, and management loses visibility. IHL helps organisations establish structured matter management — bringing all active legal work into a coherent, trackable framework that gives you a clear picture of what is open, what is at risk, and what has been resolved. Whether your legal workload involves litigation monitoring, contract disputes, compliance matters, or business transactions, we provide the structure to manage it efficiently.

02
Drafted Monitored Enforced

Contract Lifecycle Management

Contracts are where legal risk lives and where value is lost when processes are weak. IHL manages the full contract lifecycle — from template drafting and negotiation support through execution, obligation tracking, and renewal management. We create clear workflows for contract intake and approval, maintain organised document repositories, and issue proactive alerts on key dates and expiring obligations. Companies that work with us replace reactive contract firefighting with a controlled, documented process that protects their commercial interests at every stage.

03
Accessible Senior Continuous

Outside General Counsel & Retained Legal Advisory

Not every business needs a full-time General Counsel, but every business needs senior legal judgment on commercial decisions. IHL provides outside general counsel support on a retainer or project basis — giving your management team direct access to experienced legal counsel for day-to-day advisory, contract review, regulatory guidance, and strategic legal input. We work alongside your leadership, attend key meetings, and provide the legal perspective that helps decisions get made faster and with fewer downstream problems. For companies with an existing legal team, we provide specialist capacity or senior advisory support that complements rather than replaces internal resource.

04
Controlled Transparent Accountable

Legal Spend Management & Operational Efficiency

Legal costs are often the least visible line in the operating budget — until they are not. IHL helps organisations get control of their legal spend by introducing structured processes for outside counsel instruction, matter budgeting, invoice review, and cost benchmarking. We establish clear protocols for when external advice is needed, at what level, and at what cost — replacing unmanaged spend with operational discipline. For organisations looking to reduce outside counsel dependency over time, we help plan the transition to a more cost-effective legal support model.
