Our Dispute Resolution Services

Negotiations

IHL guides clients through high-stakes negotiations, helping you protect your interests and achieve constructive, mutually beneficial outcomes. Our approach combines deep legal knowledge with commercial pragmatism — ensuring that every negotiation strategy is calibrated to your specific situation, timeline, and objectives. We represent clients across corporate, commercial, and cross-border matters, bringing the preparation, discipline, and judgment that complex negotiations demand.

2
Neutrality Flexibility Understanding
Mediation

The firm provides legal representation across family law and personal injury cases to real estate and corporate disputes. Mediation offers a confidential and cost-effective path to resolution outside of the courtroom, preserving relationships and enabling parties to reach creative agreements not available through litigation. IHL supports clients at every stage of the mediation process — from preparation and strategy through to drafting enforceable settlement agreements.

3
Precision Reliability Resolution
Arbitration

Arbitration is more than just a practice area — it's at the heart of who we are. We bring deep expertise to every arbitration matter, representing clients before leading arbitral institutions and ad hoc tribunals across a wide range of sectors and jurisdictions. From procedural strategy and document production through to oral hearings and enforcement of awards, IHL provides comprehensive arbitration representation built on precision, reliability, and a commitment to achieving decisive results.

4
Expertise Insight Clarity
Expert Opinion

We deliver highly specialised legal opinions in Iranian law, Islamic law and other Sharia-based legal systems. Our expert opinions are used in arbitration proceedings, court litigation, regulatory matters, and transactional due diligence where specialist knowledge of these legal frameworks is required. IHL's expert work is characterised by rigorous analysis, clear reasoning, and opinions that withstand scrutiny from courts, tribunals, and opposing counsel.

5
Justice Equality Trust
International Litigation

Specialising in cross-border litigation, helping businesses and individuals enforce their rights, defend claims and resolve disputes efficiently in foreign courts. International litigation demands not only legal expertise but also an understanding of procedural differences, jurisdictional strategy, and the practical realities of enforcing judgments across borders. IHL coordinates complex multi-jurisdictional cases, working alongside local counsel to deliver coherent, effective litigation strategies for our clients.
