Common Questions

Coffee Industry Legal FAQ

Key legal questions for coffee businesses navigating international markets and growth.

How can I protect my brand from being copied in international markets?

Trademark registration in each target market is the foundation. We develop a proactive IP strategy that covers registration, monitoring, and enforcement — so you can act quickly if a competitor copies your brand identity or product names.

What should I do if a distributor breaches our contract?

Document everything immediately and seek legal advice before taking action. We assess your options — from direct negotiation to formal arbitration — and pursue the path most likely to recover your losses and preserve the relationship if appropriate.

What compliance requirements apply to coffee equipment exports?

Equipment exports are subject to safety certifications, energy efficiency standards, and labelling requirements that vary significantly by market. We review the specific regulations in each destination country and ensure your products meet all requirements before export.

How can I safely expand into a new international market?

Market entry requires a structured review of local trade regulations, entity setup options, contract requirements, and compliance obligations. We build a market entry plan that identifies risks early and structures your presence for long-term stability.

Why is ESG important for coffee companies today?

ESG commitments are increasingly required by major buyers, investors, and regulators — particularly in the EU. Beyond compliance, strong ESG credentials differentiate your brand and open access to premium markets. We help you build frameworks that are both credible and commercially viable.

Can IHL help with sourcing agreements from producing countries?

Yes. Sourcing agreements across multiple jurisdictions require careful attention to payment terms, quality standards, force majeure provisions, and ethical sourcing clauses. We draft agreements that protect your supply chain while reflecting your sustainability commitments.
